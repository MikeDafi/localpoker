# LocalPoker Support

Need help with LocalPoker: Poker with Friends? This support page is for account, gameplay, privacy, and safety questions about the free, play-money app.

## Contact

Email support at [maskndafi@gmail.com](mailto:maskndafi@gmail.com).

For a solo indie app, a personal mailbox is a reasonable published developer contact. If LocalPoker grows, a dedicated support alias is the usual next step.

Include:

- the device model and iOS version;
- the app version, if visible in the build you are using;
- a short description of what happened;
- screenshots only if they do not include private room codes or other sensitive details.

## Privacy and deletion requests

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email [maskndafi@gmail.com](mailto:maskndafi@gmail.com) and include any relevant anonymous Firebase UID, room code, or device profile details if you have them. Do not send passwords or payment information. LocalPoker is play-money only and does not support deposits, withdrawals, cash prizes, or real-world value transfer.

LocalPoker is intended for adults 18 and older. It uses virtual chips only. It does not offer real-money gambling, cash prizes, purchases of chips, deposits, withdrawals, cash-outs, or redemption for anything of value.
