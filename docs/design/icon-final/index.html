v5 — what changed

  • ✓ The hand is spaced. In v4 the two cards touched, so they merged into one wide white slab and the 2's left pip column was hidden. Separating them gives each card its own silhouette and reopens the centre line so the chin and mouth read. It also measurably improves the icon small — in the ladder below v5's cards stay countable as two cards down to 60 px, where v4's fuse into one shape.
  • ✓ Rim light on the head. The face met the dark felt with no separation and its silhouette dissolved when shrunk; a warm sliver along the lit edge reads as the overhead spot catching it.
  • ✓ A specular streak on one lens — the only cue that the glasses are glass rather than two black holes.
  • ✓ Corner vignette, so the tile has a focal centre instead of reading as a flat rectangle of dark green.
  • Verified: 0% of card pixels clipped by the iOS squircle in either version.

v4 — what changed from v1

  • ✓ Kept v1's face and hair. The brow-bar sunglasses, the deadpan mouth bar and the rounded hair mass are v1's — not the wayfarer/clipped treatment from v2–v3.
  • ✓ Hair now fits the top of the head. This was the one real fault in v1: its hair was an arc peaking at y = 139 while the skull topped out at y = 186, so it floated roughly 47 px proud of the head like a wig. v4 clips the head's own circle, so the hair cannot sit anywhere but on the skull.
  • ✓ Person bigger. Head radius 262 → 300, filling far more of the tile.
  • ✓ Cards bigger — and lowered so they no longer cover the mouth, since the expression is the whole point.
  • ✓ 10♥ and 2♠, offsuit — one red, one black — instead of a pair of aces.
  • ✓ Rank and suit mirrored into opposite corners on both cards: upright at the top-left, rotated 180° at the bottom-right, the way a real card is printed.
  • ✓ The cards are real cards. They are the English-pattern 10♥ and 2♠ faces by Dmitry Fomin, released CC0 / public domain on Wikimedia Commons, placed and scaled rather than redrawn. A render of the extracted face was diffed against the source: maximum channel delta 0 — pixel-identical.
  • ✓ Cards now cast a shadow so they sit in front of the face instead of looking pasted on. Built from stacked translucent shapes because feDropShadow is silently ignored by several SVG rasterisers — the first attempt rendered nothing at all.

Still not perfect

Hand-drawing the cards was the wrong approach, and not obviously so. My versions were wrong in ways that are hard to catch by eye: a 10 needs ten pips in a set arrangement (one central pip is how an ace is printed), card faces are 360×540 (2:3) rather than the squarer shape I had guessed, pips are taller than wide, and the "10" index is condensed with separately kerned digits. Ten correctly-drawn pips still cost small-size clarity — at 40 px they read as speckle rather than a countable ten — but that is now a property of real cards, not of my drawing.
10-2 is an in-joke. It reads as "confident player holding garbage", which lands with a poker player and is invisible to everyone else. A stranger browsing the App Store just sees two cards.
Dark ground still costs pop. v1's blue tile is louder among colourful home-screen neighbours; v4 looks more premium and matches the app's felt, but recedes more.
Still says nothing about friends. Unchanged, and unsolved by any single-character concept in this exploration.
