What this tells us

  • Dark was the right call. The three references average 19% lightness and 18% saturation. Our felt sits at 11% lightness and 20% saturation — the same family, slightly darker and a little more desaturated than the middle of the pack.
  • Not one of them uses a bright green cloth. They run near-black (Prominence, Pure Hold'em) or desaturated navy (Poker Club). The old #2FA35E Wii-green had no equivalent in any modern poker title.
  • Every felt carries a texture. A tonal suit watermark, a damask swirl, or a visible cloth weave — always only a few percent off the base colour. Ours is still a flat gradient; that is the clearest remaining gap.
  • Ornament is a single metallic accent. Gold line-art on black (Prominence) is the only decoration on the surface — consistent with keeping the palette restrained.
  • One light source, strong falloff. All three are lit from above with the table edges falling into shadow, which is exactly the room → rail → felt hierarchy we built.
